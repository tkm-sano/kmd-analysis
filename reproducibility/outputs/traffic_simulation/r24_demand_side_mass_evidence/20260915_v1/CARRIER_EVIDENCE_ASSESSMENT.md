# Carrier evidence assessment

Public official/technical materials from Yamato Transport, Sagawa Express and Japan Post were searched for observed parcel mass distributions.

| Carrier | Public evidence found | Observed mass distribution | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yamato Transport | service size/weight eligibility and shipment-count tools/materials | none found | `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` |
| Sagawa Express | service size/weight eligibility | none found | `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` |
| Japan Post | Yu-Pack/Yu-Packet service limits and annual handling counts | none found | `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` |

These sources establish what a service accepts, not what residential B2C customers actually receive. A tariff threshold, maximum permissible weight, size class or annual parcel count is not an empirical probability distribution and cannot be used as `F_W`.

The Ogawa demonstration used operational data from three carriers and is evaluated separately as the closest public domestic proxy. Carrier names do not make its daily aggregate chart a parcel-level distribution.

Reviewed official entry points:

- https://www.kuronekoyamato.co.jp/ytc/customer/send/services/takkyubin/
- https://www.sagawa-exp.co.jp/service/takuhai/
- https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/you_pack/
- https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/send/domestic/delivery/yu-packet/
- https://www.mlit.go.jp/report/press/jidosha04_hh_000341.html

Conclusion: **no reviewed carrier-public source provides raw observations, histogram, CDF, quantiles, mean/variance pair, or a fitted distribution for Japanese residential B2C parcel gross mass.**
