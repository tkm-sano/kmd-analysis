## Why Gate C is not closed

The review found neither a direct target-population distribution nor a close proxy with a usable public distribution. The Ogawa evidence has close delivery semantics but only daily aggregate charts. Air-cargo means, logistics-census shipment weights, cage loads, foreign bins and carrier maximums each fail at least one decisive population/denominator/distribution condition.

No `F_W`, mean weight, parametric family, truncation rule, `q_i`, `Q`, or `m` is adopted.

## Fallback and dependency

`RECOMMENDED_FALLBACK = FALLBACK_C`

R24 should separate:

1. an explicitly methodological capacity-active benchmark, based only on a later transparent contract; and
2. a deferred physical kg scenario that remains unavailable until population-valid parcel-mass evidence is obtained.

This avoids pretending that parcel-equivalent count is a manufacturer-defined physical capacity or that a foreign/air-freight mean is an Ota B2C distribution.

Routing compatibility revalidation remains required after the vehicle configuration is frozen, but it is not the next dependency: the capacity contract must first state whether the primary R24 scenario is methodological and what non-physical claims are forbidden.
