# Academic and research evidence assessment

## Closest domestic operational study

The MLIT Ogawa-district demonstration reports 33 days of deliveries by three parcel carriers, excludes inter-company transport, and says carriers supplied daily parcel count/type/size/weight data. This is close to consumer last-mile delivery semantics. However, the public report shows daily aggregate count and weight charts; it does not publish parcel records, numerical mass bins, CDF, moments or a fitted distribution. The small rural study is also not representative of dense Ota delivery. Classification: `CLOSE_PROXY_EVIDENCE`, unusable for `F_W`.

## Other Japanese evidence

- The 2025 roll-box-pallet field study directly weighed 1,012 loaded cages at logistics centers and a supermarket. Its empirical unit is the cage load, not a parcel, and parcel counts/composition are unavailable. `INCOMPATIBLE_POPULATION`.
- The SIP-adus material's 11.8 kg value is a secondary modeling value derived from older freight evidence; it is not a current residential B2C parcel sample and supplies no distribution. `WEAK_PROXY_EVIDENCE`.
- Japanese packaging/shock studies address handling and package response. They do not publish a target-population parcel-mass distribution. `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`.

## Foreign evidence

International cross-border shopper surveys and foreign marketplace studies can contain bins or summary quantiles, but country, channel, product mix, customs and self-reporting differences prevent automatic transfer to Japanese domestic residential B2C. Such evidence may support a separately labeled sensitivity analysis, not the R24 primary physical scenario.

No reviewed peer-reviewed or institutional source satisfies all of: Japanese residential B2C, parcel-level gross mass, documented sampling frame, and an accessible distribution suitable for sampling.
