# Physical versus methodological capacity

## Physical capacity scenario

Requires an authority-backed unit on both sides. The leading path is kg: official kei-van payload plus a population-matched Japanese residential B2C actual-gross-mass source, a parcel-denominator contract and a building aggregation rule. This scenario is not yet authorized.

## Methodological capacity-active scenario

A controlled count/LU capacity could test solver behavior by choosing integer demands and Q. Such values would be `METHODOLOGICAL_CAPACITY`, not physical vehicle capacity, not Ota operational evidence and not the R24 primary physical scenario. It must use a distinct scenario ID and cannot reuse the manufacturer's kg payload numerically.

R24 does not require capacity to bind. Artificially scaling `q_i` or lowering `Q` to force binding is prohibited. The two scenario types may coexist later but must never share unlabeled parameters or claims.
