# Researcher arbitrariness register

| Choice | Risk | Why | Pre-fix method |
|---|---|---|---|
| R24 formulation | HIGH | Capacity-only is trivial | Approve B with two trips before implementation |
| n | HIGH | Statevector grows exponentially | Gate n=2–3; n=4 conditional; n=5–6 estimate only |
| customer sample | HIGH | Selection can favor results | SHA-ranked sample and freeze before results |
| demand values | HIGH | No observed customer demand | New authority record with unit, transform, seed, bounds |
| capacity | HIGH | Payload is kg while demand is proxy units | Same-unit normalized Q or parcel-mass authority |
| trips/vehicles | HIGH | Changes feasibility and cost | K=2; new K is new protocol version |
| encoding | MEDIUM | Encoding changes resource and penalty behavior | Select encoding 1 and independently verify fixture |
| penalty | HIGH | Can manufacture feasible optima | Derive sufficient bound; otherwise stop |
| p | MEDIUM | Affects optimization outcome | Freeze p before run, resource-gated |
| optimizer | MEDIUM | Success semantics vary | Freeze optimizer, budget, and success definition |
| initialization | MEDIUM | Sensitivity is material in R23 | Freeze deterministic initialization and seed |
| stopping rule | HIGH | Budget hit can be misread as convergence | Freeze cap and report termination separately |
