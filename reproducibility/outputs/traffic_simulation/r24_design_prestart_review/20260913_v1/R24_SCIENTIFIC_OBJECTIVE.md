# R24 scientific objective

R24 extends the accepted R23 route-ordering problem with the smallest additional structure that makes capacity operationally meaningful: two depot-returning trips by one vehicle. It will test, using independently generated classical exact references, how customer demand and per-trip capacity change customer grouping, total travel cost, feasible-state probability, and logical-qubit growth while preserving R23 route semantics.

The study is about formulation and small-scale validation. It will not claim fleet sizing, EV performance, quantum advantage, or real delivery operations.
