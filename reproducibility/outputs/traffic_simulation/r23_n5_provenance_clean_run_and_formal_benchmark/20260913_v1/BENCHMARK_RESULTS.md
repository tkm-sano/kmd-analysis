# Benchmark results

All six control runs returned identical objective, route, P_feasible and P_optimal values within the reported precision. Mean total wall time was 1.2161 s (original) versus 1.1966 s (v4), an observed ratio of about 1.016. Mean postprocess time was 0.007236 s versus 0.000407 s. Peak RSS was 107520–113664 KiB for both implementations. These are controlled n=3 observations; no n=5 full speedup or memory factor is promoted.
